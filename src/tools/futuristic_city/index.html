💡 How to Use
  • Rotate: Click and drag to rotate the camera around the city
  • Zoom: Use mouse wheel to zoom in and out
  • Time of Day: Adjust the slider to experience sunrise, midday, sunset, and night
  • Fog Density: Increase fog for a mysterious atmosphere, decrease for clarity
  • Animation Speed: Control how fast cars and people move through the city

🏙️ City Features

🏢 Procedural Buildings

The city features dynamically generated skyscrapers with varying heights, colors, and architectural details. Each building has illuminated windows that respond to the day/night cycle, and taller buildings feature antennas with blinking beacons for added realism.

🚗 Dynamic Traffic

Watch cars navigate through the city's highway network. Each vehicle has headlights and taillights that automatically turn on at night, creating a beautiful display of moving lights during the evening hours.

◈ Orbital Skyways

Elevated luminous rails encircle the inner districts while autonomous aerial craft move on independent routes above the street grid and beneath floating navigation sigils.

🌅 Day/Night Cycle

Experience the city at any time of day. Watch the sun move across the sky, see shadows shift, and witness the transformation as buildings light up at night. The atmospheric effects include dynamic sky colors, realistic lighting, and adjustable fog.
