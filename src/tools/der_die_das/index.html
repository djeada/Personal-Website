🇩🇪 Der Die Das

Learn German articles by guiding falling words to the correct container!

  • Use ← → arrow keys to move words left or right
  • Press Space or ↓ to drop words faster
  • On mobile, drag the word left/right to aim
  • Drop each word into the correct article container
  • Tap the word to reveal meaning
  • Build streaks for bonus points
  • Speed increases as you level up!

Easy = A1/A2 words. Medium = A1/A2 + B1/B2. Hard = all words.
