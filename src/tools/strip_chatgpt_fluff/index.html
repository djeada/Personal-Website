Need Help?
  • Paste or type your AI-generated text into the input editor.
  • Drag and drop text files (.txt, .md) to load them directly.
  • Click "Process Text" or press Ctrl+Enter to write cleaned text to the output editor.
  • Use the sidebar options to customize which transformations are applied.
  • Click preset buttons (Safe Cleanup/Markdown/Publish Polish/Aggressive) for quick configurations.
  • Use the Search & Replace option to find and replace specific text patterns.
  • Track character, word, and line counts in the stats bar.
  • Use Ctrl+Z to undo and Ctrl+Y to redo changes.
  • Click "Copy" to copy the output or "Download" to save it as a text file.
  • Review the change report after processing to see exactly what was modified.
  • View your editing history in the collapsible History panel.
