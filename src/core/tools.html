Optics — Classical Learning Path

Geometrical Optics Bench

Ray tracing with ABCD matrices and exact Snell refraction: lenses, mirrors, stops, pupils, telescopes, microscopes and aberrations. Start here.

Electromagnetic Waves and Energy Flow

Plane-wave E and H fields, wave impedance and the Poynting vector. Prerequisite: vectors, complex numbers.

Fresnel Interface Coefficients

Reflection and refraction at an interface: complex r and t, Brewster angle, total internal reflection and evanescent waves. After: EM waves.

Polarization and Jones/Mueller Calculus

Polarization states, an ordered bench of polarizers and wave plates, Stokes parameters and the Poincaré sphere. After: Fresnel.

Interference and Beating

Superposition of two waves, coherence, polarization overlap, visibility and temporal beats. After: EM waves.

Standing Waves

Counter-propagating waves, reflection phase, standing-wave ratio and two-mirror resonator modes. After: interference, Fresnel.

Interferometers and Coherence

Michelson and Mach–Zehnder interferometers, finite source bandwidth, coherence length and fringe visibility. After: interference.

Thin Films and Multilayer Coatings

Anti-reflection coatings, Bragg mirrors and absorbing films with transfer matrices. After: Fresnel, interference.

Double-Slit Experiment

Fraunhofer two-slit interference with finite slit width, partial coherence and photon build-up. After: interference.

Single-Slit and Circular Diffraction

Far-field slit and Airy patterns on a calibrated detector, Fresnel number and enclosed power. After: double slit.

Near- and Far-Field Aperture Propagation

Numerical angular-spectrum and Fresnel propagation of arbitrary apertures: zone plates, Poisson spot and sampling limits. After: diffraction.

Diffraction Gratings and Spectroscopy

N-slit gratings, orders, resolving power and a spectrometer with slit and pixel limits. After: diffraction.

Fourier Optics, Imaging and Resolution

4f systems, spatial filtering, PSF, MTF, coherent vs incoherent imaging and Zernike aberrations. After: geometrical optics, aperture propagation.

Radiometry, Throughput and Detection

Power budgets, étendue, radiance conservation, photon counting, shot noise and SNR. After: geometrical optics.
💡

Photonics — Advanced Path

Gaussian Beams

Waist, Rayleigh range, Gouy phase, complex q and ABCD beam transformation, focusing and mode matching. After: geometrical optics, diffraction.

Fabry–Pérot and Passive Resonators

Free spectral range, finesse, photon lifetime, g1–g2 stability and transverse modes. After: standing waves, Gaussian beams.

Laser Cavity and Rate Equations

Four-level rate equations, threshold, L–I curve and relaxation oscillations. After: Fabry–Pérot.

Optical Materials, Dispersion and Pulses

Sellmeier and Lorentz models, group velocity, GVD and ultrashort-pulse broadening and chirp. After: EM waves.

Waveguides and Optical Fibres

Slab TE/TM modes, step-index fibre LP modes, V-number, cutoff, modal dispersion and coupling. After: Fresnel, dispersion.
🧪

Optics — Specialist Extensions

Holography and Phase Retrieval

Record and reconstruct holograms: off-axis order separation, twin images, phase shifting and Gerchberg–Saxton. After: aperture propagation, Fourier optics.

Nonlinear Optics

Second-harmonic generation, phase matching, quasi-phase matching and self-phase modulation with a split-step solver. After: dispersion.

Quantum Optics and Photon Statistics

Single-photon interference with which-path information, photon-number statistics and g²(τ) bunching and antibunching. After: interferometers, radiometry.

Electro- and Acousto-Optic Modulators

Pockels phase modulators, Mach–Zehnder modulators, acousto-optic Bragg cells and directional couplers. After: polarization, waveguides.
